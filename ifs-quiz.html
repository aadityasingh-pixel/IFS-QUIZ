📚 Topics to Study

    📜 IFS Core Cheat Sheet

    🏦 Repo Rate vs Reverse Repo Rate:Repo Rate is the rate at which RBI lends money to commercial banks against government securities. Reverse Repo is when RBI borrows/absorbs liquidity from commercial banks.
    📜 Treasury Bills (T-Bills):Issued ONLY by the Central Government. Short-term debt instruments issued at a discount to face value and redeemed at par. Maturities: 91, 182, 364 days.
    💼 Commercial Paper (CP):Unsecured money market instrument issued by highly-rated corporations to raise short-term capital. Minimum tenure: 7 days, maximum 1 year.
    🛡️ SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India):Established as a statutory body in 1992 to protect investor interests, regulate securities markets, and prevent insider trading.
